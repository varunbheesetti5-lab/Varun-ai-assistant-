## 2025-05-14 - Accessible Icon-Only Buttons
**Learning:** Icon-only buttons (like 'Send' or 'Copy') are common in chat interfaces but are inaccessible to screen readers without an ARIA label. Adding a 'title' attribute alongside 'aria-label' provides a consistent tooltip for mouse users while ensuring accessibility.
**Action:** Always pair 'aria-label' with 'title' for icon-only buttons to enhance both accessibility and discoverability.
